# Communication strategy

Required by the brief: the audience and situation, the barrier, the key message and its proof,
the role of channels; promises match the product. Written after the product strategy and derived
from it.

- **Audience and situation.** The same segment as the product strategy, and the moment you
  reach it (before purchase, at renewal, after a failure).
- **Barrier.** The one thing that stops the audience. Take it from leavers, reviews or
  research, as a claim; a guessed barrier is a hypothesis.
- **Key message.** One sentence that removes the barrier.
- **Proof.** Why the audience should believe it: a product capability, a number, a comparison.
  Proof is a claim.
- **Role of channels.** For each channel, its job (reach, explain, remind, retain) and why it
  fits the audience. No channel lists without jobs.

## Promise check

List every promise the message and channels make. Each must map to a product capability that
exists or is in the product strategy's direction, or to a claim. A promise with no mapping is
removed or rewritten. Record the check as the table in the template.
