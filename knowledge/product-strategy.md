# Product strategy

Required by the brief: the audience and its task, the alternatives, the direction and the
refusals, the value against cost and revenue. About one page. It follows from the option the
user chose at Gate 2.

- **Audience and its task.** A segment you can find in the data (a cohort, a channel, a plan),
  and the job it hires the product for. Not "everyone".
- **Alternatives.** What the audience uses instead today, including doing nothing and the
  competitors from the research, with their price and offer as claims.
- **Direction.** Three to five moves for the next quarter, each tied to a claim or a gap.
- **Refusals.** What you will stop or not start, and why. A strategy without refusals is a wish
  list.
- **Value against cost and revenue.** What the user gains, what it costs to build and run, how
  revenue moves. Numbers are claims; your estimates are `forecast` claims and say so.
- **Risks and assumptions.** The assumptions the strategy rests on, each as a claim.
