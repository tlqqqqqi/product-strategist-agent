# Verification plan

Required by the brief: the expected change, the comparison, the observation period, the
unwanted effects.

- **Expected change.** One primary metric, the direction and size you expect, by when. The
  baseline is a claim.
- **Comparison.** Against what: a control group, the period before, another segment. Say why
  the comparison isolates the decision from other changes (seasonality, other releases).
- **Observation period.** Long enough for the metric to react: a monthly churn metric needs at
  least one full billing cycle after the change.
- **Unwanted effects.** Guardrail metrics that could get worse (revenue, support load,
  complaints, another segment) and the threshold that stops the change.
- **Review conditions.** The result that makes you revisit the decision; these go into the
  decision record.
